Getting started

Start with the first level. Try the buttons or joystick and adjust the control size in the options. Use the double jump to cross wider gaps. Earn stars by finishing a level, finding all three lost laughs and completing the course quickly.

Bosses and obstacles

Bosses include Tronco Zangado, Faraó Mumificado and Nuvem Trovão. The six worlds feature rising lava, slippery ice, gusts of wind and falling platforms.
